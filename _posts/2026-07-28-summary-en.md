## [llama.cpp b10153 Adds Nanbeige4.2 Support and Cross-Platform Binaries](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b10153) ⭐️ 9.0/10

llama.cpp version b10153 introduces support for the Nanbeige4.2 AI model and provides pre-built binaries for macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, and Windows. This release expands the ecosystem of locally runnable AI models, enabling developers to run Nanbeige4.2 on diverse hardware without compiling from source. The update includes loop bound checks, lint fixes, and disables KleidiAI for macOS Apple Silicon due to compatibility issues, while maintaining support for CUDA, Vulkan, ROCm, and other backends.

Pirate Face is a project that uses torrent-based distribution and technical workarounds to rescue AI models, particularly uncensored models, from deletion. This initiative addresses the issue of model censorship and single points of failure in centralized distribution platforms, offering a decentralized alternative for preserving AI research. The project leverages BitTorrent for distribution and explores methods like orthogonalising activations to bypass censorship, though it lacks scripted torrent creation tools.

**Background**: LLM models are often subject to censorship or deletion by hosting platforms, and decentralized distribution methods like BitTorrent can help preserve them. Orthogonalising activations is a technique to modify model behavior without altering its weights.

**Discussion**: Users discuss the efficiency of torrents for model distribution, compare it to historical game distribution methods, and debate the technical equivalence of abliterated weights versus orthogonalising activations.

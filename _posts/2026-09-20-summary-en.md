The llama.cpp project has released version b11050, which corresponds to v0.5.0, featuring fixes for Metal support on macOS and providing a wide array of pre-built binaries for various operating systems and hardware architectures. This release is significant because it enhances the usability of llama.cpp on Apple Silicon devices and ensures that users can run large language models efficiently across a diverse range of platforms, from macOS and Linux to Windows and Android.
